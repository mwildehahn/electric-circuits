Summary: External consumers pin change-log segments with a durable position, and the retention sweep treats them like dormant shapes: pinned segments are kept, stale consumers are evicted before their segments go.
## Interface
- Engine routes: `PUT /consumers/{consumer_id}` with `{ "segment": n, "offset": "..." }` records the consumer’s durable position (idempotent, monotonic: a position behind the recorded one is refused with 409); `GET /consumers` lists consumers with position, pinned segments, and lag; `DELETE /consumers/{consumer_id}` releases the pin. Under circuitsd: `/sources/{source_id}/consumers[/{consumer_id}]`.
- Catalog events `ConsumerPinned { id, position }` and `ConsumerEvicted { id }` persist pins across restarts.
## Guarantees
- `plan_segment_deletion` computes its floor as the minimum over the durable sequencer checkpoint, dormant-shape resume positions, and every external consumer position.
- A consumer whose position is older than `ELECTRIC_CIRCUITS_CHANGES_RETAIN_SECS` is evicted first (recorded as `ConsumerEvicted`), and only the segments that eviction unpinned are deleted in that sweep; an eviction that fails defers.
- An evicted consumer’s next `PUT` is accepted only at or after the oldest retained segment; its next read of a deleted segment is 410.
- Pins survive restart: a consumer registered before a restart still pins after it.
## Constraints
- Pins never delay rotation, only deletion.
- A consumer id is caller-chosen and namespaced per source; two sources never share consumer state.
## Verifications
- Pin holds: consumer at segment 0, sequencer checkpoint in segment 2, sweep -> segments 0 and 1 remain; advance the consumer to segment 2, sweep -> 0 and 1 are deleted.
- Retain-window eviction: consumer pinned beyond the retain window, sweep -> `ConsumerEvicted` recorded, segments deleted, `GET /consumers` no longer lists it, the consumer’s read returns 410.
- Monotonic positions: a `PUT` behind the recorded position -> 409 and the pin is unchanged.
- Restart: register, restart the engine from its catalog, sweep -> the pin still holds.
